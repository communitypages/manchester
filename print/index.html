How to print a list

You can print a list of activities from Community Pages for someone who isn’t online, or put one up on a noticeboard. It’s free, and you don’t need to ask first.

1. Choose the area

On the Find activities page, type a postcode (for example M14 or SK4) and choose how far, from 1 to 10 miles. You can also type a place name, like Salford or Chorlton, in the search box.

2. Choose the dates

Use the dates menu: Today, Tomorrow, This weekend, Next 7 days, Next 30 days, or pick a date.

3. Pick a type of activity (optional)

Type what you’re looking for in the search box, like walking, coffee or craft, to only print those.

4. Press Print this list

Press the 🖨️ Print this list button near the top of the page. Your printer options will open. Choose your printer, or Save as PDF to email it or print it later. On a phone or tablet, it opens your phone’s print or share options.

Go to Find activities

What you get

  • An A4 list in two columns, grouped by day, with one-off events at the end.
  • The time, venue, cost and a contact for each activity.
  • A QR code and the website address, so people can look up more later.
  • The date it was printed.

Tips

  • Keep it short. All of Greater Manchester for a week runs to many pages. A postcode, 1 mile and the next 7 days is usually about 2 pages.
  • For a noticeboard, print your local area for the next 30 days and swap it each month.
  • For one person, print what’s near them, or just the kind of thing they’re interested in.
