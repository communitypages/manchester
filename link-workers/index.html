For link workers and support staff

Community Pages lists free, donation and £5-or-less activities across Greater Manchester – coffee mornings, walking groups, ESOL, job clubs, wellbeing groups, stay and play and more. It’s free to use, with no login.

Find activities near someone

  1. Go to communitypages.org.uk.
  2. Type their postcode (or just the first part, e.g. M19) and choose a distance, from 1 to 10 miles.
  3. Choose when: today, this weekend, the next 7 or 30 days, or a date.
  4. Use the search box to narrow it down, e.g. “walk”, “ESOL”, “coffee” or a place name.

Each listing shows the time, venue, cost, and whether people can drop in or need to book first. Tap a listing for details and contact information.

Print a list

Set the postcode, distance and dates, then press Print this list. You get a short A4 list with times, venues, costs and a contact for each activity – useful for people without internet access, or for a noticeboard.

Please remind people to check before they go. Groups sometimes change times or stop running, and we only hear about it if someone tells us.

Help keep it accurate

You often hear first when a group stops or changes. Please email us with the group’s name and what’s changed.

Know a free or low-cost group that isn’t listed? Send it to hello@communitypages.org.uk.
