Add your event

Listing is free. We list activities across Greater Manchester that are open to the public and free, donation-based or £5 or less. Regular groups and one-off events are both welcome.

Email us your events

Press the button to open an email with a short list to fill in. Add as many events as you like, or attach a timetable or flyer.

Email us your event

If the button doesn’t open your email, send the details to hello@communitypages.org.uk.

What to include for each event

  • Name of the event and your group
  • When it happens – the day and how often, or the date – and the start and end times
  • Venue name and address, with postcode
  • Cost: free, donation, or the price (£5 or less)
  • Whether people can just turn up or need to book
  • A phone number or email for enquiries (this is shown on the website)
  • A website or social media link, if you have one

What happens next

We check every entry by hand. It usually appears on the site within a week. We don’t list political campaigns, religious worship or business promotion.

Already listed and something has changed, or the group has stopped? Email hello@communitypages.org.uk so we can update it.
