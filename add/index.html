Add your event

Listing is free. We list activities across Greater Manchester that are free, donation-based or £5 or less.

Email us your event

Or email hello@communitypages.org.uk with, for each event:

  • Name of the event and your group
  • When it happens (and whether it repeats) and the times
  • Venue address with postcode
  • Cost, and whether people can just turn up or need to book
  • A phone number or email for enquiries

A timetable or flyer attached is fine too.
