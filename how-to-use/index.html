How to use Community Pages

Find free and low-cost activities near you, save them to your calendar, share them and print a list for someone who isn’t online. It’s free, and you don’t need an account.

Search for activities

  • On the Find activities page, type what you’re looking for, like coffee, walking, baby or ESOL. You can also type a place, like Salford or Chorlton.
  • Add your postcode to see what’s near you. A full postcode (for example M16 0AB) gives the most accurate distances.
  • Press Search. You can then choose the dates (Today, Tomorrow, This weekend, Next 7 days, Next 30 days, or pick a date) and how far, from 1 to 10 miles.
  • You can also use the menu to see only Christmas or 50+ activities.

See the details

Tap or click an activity to open it. You’ll see the address, times, cost and who to contact.

Save to your calendar

Open the activity and press Save to my calendar. This downloads a small calendar file. Open it and it adds the activity to your phone or computer calendar (Google, Apple or Outlook). For weekly groups, it saves the date you’re looking at.

Share with someone

Open the activity and press Share. On a phone, this opens your share options, so you can send it by WhatsApp, text or email. On a computer, it copies the details, ready to paste into an email or message.

Print a list

You can print a list for someone who isn’t online, or put one up on a noticeboard. You don’t need to ask first.

  1. Choose the area: type a postcode (for example M14 or SK4) or a place name, then press Search.
  2. Choose the dates and, if you like, a type of activity, like walking or craft.
  3. Press 🖨️ Print this list near the top of the page. It appears after you press Search. Choose your printer, or Save as PDF to email it or print it later. On a phone or tablet, it opens your phone’s print or share options.

You get an A4 list in two columns, grouped by day, with the time, venue, cost and a contact for each activity, plus a QR code and the website address.

Tips: keep it short. A postcode, 1 mile and the next 7 days is usually about 2 pages. For a noticeboard, print your local area for the next 30 days and swap it each month.

Spotted something wrong?

Open the activity and press Edit. This starts an email to us with the details filled in, so you just say what’s changed. To add a new activity, see Add an event.

Share our flyer

Help more people find Community Pages. Print our A5 flyer for a noticeboard, waiting room or reception desk, or send it on by email. It has a QR code that opens the website.
