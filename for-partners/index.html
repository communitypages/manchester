Show local activities on your website

Put a live list of free and low-cost local activities on your own website. It’s for housing associations, GP surgeries, libraries, community centres, councils and charities. It updates itself every day from Community Pages, so there’s nothing for you to do once it’s on your site.

See it working See prices

Why add it

  • Help your tenants, patients and visitors find something to do nearby: coffee mornings, walking groups, craft clubs, ESOL classes, stay and play, lunch clubs and more.
  • Every activity is free, donation-based or £5 or less. Nobody needs a referral. People get in touch with the group or just turn up.
  • Updated every day. Every listing is checked by hand before it goes on Community Pages, and the list on your site changes when ours does. Groups do change their plans, so the list asks people to check with the organiser before they go.
  • Shows only what you want. Choose a borough or neighbourhood, and the types of activity that suit the people you work with.
  • Works on phones. It matches the Community Pages look, and finishes with a small “Powered by Community Pages” link.

Try it

Choose an area and a type of activity to see what the list would look like on your site.

How it goes on your site

When your licence starts, we send you a short piece of code to paste into your web page and switch the list on for your website. Most website builders (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and council or housing association systems) have an “Embed” or “Custom HTML” block for this, and it only takes a few minutes. We’ll help if you get stuck.

Licence prices

A licence lets your organisation show the live activities list on your website. Every licence covers all of Greater Manchester, and you choose which areas and activities to show. Prices are per year.

  • Small charity, community group or community centre: £60 a year
  • GP practice, library or medium-sized charity: £150 a year
  • Housing association, council or large organisation: £300 a year

Every licence includes:

  • the list on as many pages of your website as you need
  • any areas and activity types across Greater Manchester
  • help setting it up and checking it looks right
  • daily updates, with each listing checked by hand

Community Pages is run by a volunteer, and your licence helps keep it going for everyone who uses it.

To buy a licence

Email hello@communitypages.org.uk with your organisation’s name, your website, and the areas and activities you’d like to show. We’ll send an invoice. Your list goes live once it’s paid.

We reply weekdays between 9 and 11am. We use your details only to reply to you. See our privacy notice.

Small community groups that just want to link to us don’t need a licence. Linking to communitypages.org.uk or putting up our flyer is always free.
