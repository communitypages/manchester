Show local activities on your website

Put a live list of free and low-cost local activities on your own website. It’s for housing associations, GP surgeries, libraries, community centres, councils and charities. It updates itself every day from Community Pages, so there’s nothing for you to do once it’s on your site.

See it working Ask about a licence

Why add it

  • Help your tenants, patients and visitors find something to do nearby: coffee mornings, walking groups, craft clubs, ESOL classes, stay and play, lunch clubs and more.
  • Every activity is free, donation-based or £5 or less. Nobody needs a referral. People get in touch with the group or just turn up.
  • Updated every day. Every listing is checked by hand before it goes on Community Pages, and the list on your site changes when ours does. Groups do change their plans, so the list asks people to check with the organiser before they go.
  • Shows only what you want. Choose a borough or neighbourhood, and the types of activity that suit the people you work with.
  • Works on phones. It matches the Community Pages look, and finishes with a small “Powered by Community Pages” link.

How it goes on your site

With a licence, you paste a short piece of code into your web page. Most website builders (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and council or housing association systems) have an “Embed” or “Custom HTML” block for this. Your web team or supplier can do it in a few minutes. Licences


Community Pages is run by a volunteer, and checking every listing by hand takes time. A licence lets your organisation show the live list on your website, and it helps pay for keeping Community Pages going and free for everyone who uses it.


    

  • A licence covers your organisation’s website, with as many pages and areas as you need.

  • We’ll help you pick the right areas and activities, and check the list looks right on your site.
