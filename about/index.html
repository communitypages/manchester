About Community Pages

Community Pages is a free website listing local activities and events across Greater Manchester that are free, donation-based or £5 or less. It covers all ten boroughs: Manchester, Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Tameside, Oldham, Rochdale, Bury, Bolton and Wigan.

It’s for anyone looking for something to do nearby – a coffee morning, a walking group, an ESOL class, a craft club – and for social prescribing link workers and others who help people find local groups.

Who runs it

Community Pages is run by one volunteer, Louise Fletcher. It’s free to use and free to be listed. If you’d like to help keep it going, you can support it on Patreon.

What we list

  • Activities open to the public that are free, donation or pay-what-you-can, or cost £5 or less.
  • Regular groups (weekly, fortnightly, monthly) and one-off events.

We don’t list political campaigns, religious worship, or business promotion. We may leave out anything we can’t check.

How listings are kept up to date

Listings come from groups and venues, and from public what’s-on pages. Each one is checked by hand before it goes on the site, and the site updates every morning. Things do change, so please check with the organiser before you go.

Add or change a listing

It’s free. Email the details to hello@communitypages.org.uk.
