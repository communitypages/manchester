About Community Pages

Community Pages is a website listing local activities and events across Greater Manchester that are free, donation-based or £5 or less. It covers all ten boroughs: Manchester, Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Tameside, Oldham, Rochdale, Bury, Bolton and Wigan.

It’s for anyone looking for something to do nearby – a coffee morning, a walking group, an ESOL class, a craft club – and for social prescribing link workers and others who help people find local groups. The aim is to reduce isolation and loneliness.

Why Community Pages exists

Louise set up Community Pages after finding it really hard to find activities costing £5 or less when she was isolated and needed something to do. Lots of good groups exist, but the information is spread across different websites, social media and noticeboards, and often split by borough. So the people who’d benefit most often never hear about them.

People don’t stick to borough boundaries, so Community Pages covers all of Greater Manchester in one place. The nearest or best group for someone might be just over the border.

It’s for anyone feeling lonely or wanting to get out more, such as new parents, retirees or people out of work. You don’t need a referral. Just get in touch with the group or turn up. The aim is to help community grow where people live, with neighbours meeting people they might never otherwise have met.

Not online? Print a list

Not everyone has a computer or the internet, so you can print any list from the website for someone, or ask at a library. Choose an area and dates, then press Print this list to get a short A4 list with times, venues, costs and contacts. GP surgeries, libraries, community centres and housing offices are welcome to print it for their noticeboards or hand copies out. How to print a list.

What we list

  • Activities open to the public that are free, donation or pay-what-you-can, or cost £5 or less.
  • Regular groups (weekly, fortnightly, monthly) and one-off events.

We don’t list political campaigns, religious worship, or business promotion. We may leave out anything we can’t check.

How listings are kept up to date

Community Pages is community led. Listings come from local people, groups and venues, and from public what’s-on pages. Anyone can add an activity. Each one is checked by hand before it goes on the site, and the site updates every day. Things do change, so please check with the organiser before you go.
