55+ activities in Greater Manchester

106 free, donation-based and £5-or-less groups for people aged 55 and over across Greater Manchester, including over-50s, 55+ and 65+ groups, Age UK and Good Neighbours activities, and dementia-friendly sessions. Many other groups on the site welcome all ages too. The age shown in each title is the group’s own.

Updated 15 November 2026. Things change, so please check with the organiser before you go.
