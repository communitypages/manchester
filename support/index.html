Our story

I’m Louise, and I started Community Pages because I needed it myself. When I was isolated, I wanted something to do and people to meet, but activities costing £5 or less were really hard to find. They were spread across noticeboards, social media and websites, and a lot of the information was out of date. When you’re already feeling low, that’s often enough to make you give up and stay at home.

In 2026 I built Community Pages: one website for low-cost community activities across all ten boroughs of Greater Manchester. It now lists 1,500 activities each week from 700 groups, including coffee mornings, walks, stay and play, singing, crafts, lunch clubs and more. It’s for anyone feeling isolated or lonely, of any age: new parents, retirees, people out of work, people new to the area. You don’t need a referral. You just find something nearby and go.

Why it matters

Loneliness affects health as much as it affects happiness. There are hundreds of brilliant community groups out there, but many people never hear about them. Small groups often don’t have the time or money to advertise, so they rely on word of mouth. Community Pages brings them together in one place, so people can find them and groups can reach new faces.

What the site does

  • Search by day, area or postcode to find activities nearby
  • Pages for older people, kids and families, and seasonal events like Christmas
  • A map of the community venues where activities take place
  • Print-friendly lists, so people without internet can still find out what’s on, and venues can put them on their noticeboards
  • Social prescribers and link workers can share it with the people they support

How we’ll use the money

Keeping Greater Manchester’s listings accurate takes at least one hour every day: checking dates, adding new groups and removing ones that have stopped. At £15 an hour, that’s £5,475 a year, and right now I do it unpaid.

We want to grow in stages:

  • Stage 1, Greater Manchester (£5,475 a year): paid admin to keep all 700 groups up to date, plus hosting and running costs.
  • Stage 2, ready to grow (about £9,000): a developer to rebuild the site so it works for many areas and lets groups add their own activities.
  • Stage 3, new areas (£5,475 a year each): paid local admin for every new area, so listings stay accurate as we grow across England.

Every pound helps keep Community Pages going for the people who need it. If you can’t donate, please share this page. It really helps.
