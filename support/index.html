Support Community Pages

Community Pages is free for everyone, and always will be. It lists around 1,500 activities a week from 700 groups across all ten boroughs of Greater Manchester – coffee mornings, walks, stay and play, singing, crafts, lunch clubs and more – all free, donation or £5 and under.

Louise runs it alone as a volunteer. Keeping the listings accurate takes at least an hour every day: checking dates, adding new groups and removing ones that have stopped.

Support our Crowdfunder

What your support pays for

  • Keeping Greater Manchester up to date: paid admin time to check all 700 groups, plus hosting and running costs.
  • A better website: a developer to rebuild the site so groups can add their own activities and it can work for more areas.
  • New areas: local admin so we can grow beyond Greater Manchester, with listings kept just as accurate.

Can’t donate? You can still help

  • Share the Crowdfunder page or the website with friends, groups and colleagues.
  • Put our flyer up on a noticeboard.
  • Run a group? Add your activity and link to communitypages.org.uk from your website.

Questions? Email hello@communitypages.org.uk.
