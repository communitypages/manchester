Support Community Pages

Community Pages is free for everyone – free to use and free to be listed. It’s run by one volunteer, with no funding, no advertising and no tracking. Every bit of help keeps it going.

Give a little each month

Small regular donations help cover the costs of running the site and the time it takes to keep listings checked and up to date.

Support us on Patreon

Help in other ways – all free

  • Tell people about it. Share communitypages.org.uk with friends, neighbours, clients or colleagues.
  • Send us your events. If you run a group, add your free or low-cost activities.
  • Tell us when something changes. If a group has stopped or moved, let us know.
  • Link to us. If your group or organisation has a website, a link to Community Pages helps more people find it.
  • Put up a printed list. Use “Print this list” on the main page for a noticeboard, waiting room or reception desk.
