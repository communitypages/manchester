Terms of use

Community Pages is free for everyone to use. These terms explain what you can do with the listings, and what needs our permission first.

You’re welcome to

  • Search the site, and save activities to your own calendar.
  • Share individual activities with friends, family, patients, tenants or clients.
  • Print lists for someone who isn’t online, or for a noticeboard in a GP surgery, library, community centre or housing office.
  • Link to any page on Community Pages from your own website or social media.

Please ask us first before you

  • Copy or republish the listings in bulk, on another website, app, directory or database, or in a newsletter.
  • Show our listings on your own website. Organisations can do this with a licence: see get in touch.
  • Collect the listings automatically with scrapers, bots or other tools.
  • Use the listings to train AI models.

Why

Community Pages is run by a volunteer. Every listing is found, checked by hand and kept up to date, which takes a lot of time. The listings as a whole are a database that belongs to Community Pages. Copies we don’t know about quickly go out of date, which means people turn up to groups that have stopped or moved.

Listings

We do our best to keep listings right, but groups change their plans. Please check with the organiser before you go. Community Pages doesn’t run the activities listed and isn’t responsible for them. Spotted something wrong? Let us know.

Contact

For permission or a licence, email hello@communitypages.org.uk. Our privacy notice explains how we use personal information.
