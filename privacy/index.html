Privacy

Community Pages is run by Louise Fletcher, a volunteer, registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO registration number ZB679223). This page explains what personal information is used and why. Questions: hello@communitypages.org.uk.

Event listings

  • Listings come from information groups have published themselves or sent to us.
  • We show a contact for each activity so people can ask about it. We only list contact details given for that purpose.
  • Listings are removed from the site after the event ends.

If you just use the website

  • There are no accounts, no tracking cookies and no advertising.
  • Your filter choices (such as a postcode you type) are kept in your browser only while the page is open. They are not sent to us.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see your device’s IP address when your browser loads the page. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy.

If you email us an event

  • We use your name and email address to check the listing and to contact you about it. We don’t share them or add you to a mailing list.
  • Contact details you ask us to show (a phone number or email for enquiries) are published on the website with the event.
  • We delete your email 12 months after your last listing ends.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, or to have a listing removed, by emailing hello@communitypages.org.uk. We’ll change or remove a listing within 7 days. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
